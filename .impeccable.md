## Design Context

### Users

Visitors exploring YC's personal portfolio and reading its writing; YC uses the
CMS to publish and preview that content.

### Brand Personality

Editorial, precise, and understated. The experience should let type and work
carry the identity rather than rely on decorative colour.

### Aesthetic Direction

A strict near-white and near-black base palette across light and dark themes,
with the original Strawberry Foam pink reserved for interactive accents.
Spacing and typography, rather than tonal variation, provide hierarchy.

### Design Principles

- Use the two core tones for surfaces; reserve Strawberry Foam for interactive
  state and editorial emphasis.
- Keep text comfortably legible in both themes.
- Preserve the existing restrained motion and reduced-motion fallbacks.
- Prefer sparse, editorial surfaces over decorative colour treatment.
